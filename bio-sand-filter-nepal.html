Bio Sand Filter in Nepal

Natural water purification for homes, farms, schools, and communities.

Safer drinking water with simple daily use

Dipak Suppliers provides bio-sand filters for households and community use in Nepal. The filter is a household version of slow sand filtration, a method used for community water treatment for more than 200 years.

Water is poured from the top and passes through sand and gravel layers. A natural biological layer develops on the sand surface and helps reduce harmful organisms, while the sand removes particles and turbidity.
